The triage agent may only answer from this file. Anything not covered here is routed to a person.

## Billing
- Customers switch between monthly and yearly billing under Settings > Billing. The change applies at the next renewal.
- Invoices are under Settings > Billing > Invoices, as PDFs.
- Refunds are decided by a person. Never promise one.

## Account
- Changing the account email, cancelling, exporting or deleting data: a person handles these after verifying identity.

## Product
- Password reset: use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The link expires after 1 hour.
