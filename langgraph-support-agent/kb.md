# Knowledge base (replace with your own)

The support agent may only answer from this file. Anything not covered here goes to a person.

## Billing
- Customers switch between monthly and yearly billing under Settings > Billing. The change applies at the next renewal.
- Invoices are under Settings > Billing > Invoices, as PDFs. Each invoice can be downloaded again at any time.
- To add a VAT number to future invoices: Settings > Billing > Tax details. Past invoices are not reissued automatically.
- Refunds are decided by a person. Never promise one, and never say one has been approved.

## Account
- Changing the account email, cancelling, exporting or deleting data: a person handles these after verifying identity.
- Adding a teammate: Settings > Team > Invite. Each workspace can have up to 10 people.

## Product
- Password reset: use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The link expires after 1 hour.
- Two-factor authentication: Settings > Security. Recovery codes are shown once, when it is turned on.
- Status page: https://status.example.com
