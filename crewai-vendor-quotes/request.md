# Request for quotation (replace with your own)

Everything the crew may tell a vendor is in this file. A vendor question it
does not answer goes to a person.

## What we are buying

- 2,000 corrugated shipping boxes, 30 x 20 x 15 cm inside, single wall, brown kraft.
- One-colour logo print on two sides.
- Delivered to our warehouse in Rotterdam, Netherlands.
- Needed by 14 November 2026.

## What each quote must state

- The unit price and the currency.
- The total for all 2,000 boxes, including any print setup.
- The lead time, from order to delivery.
- How long the quote is valid.
- Shipping to Rotterdam, or that it is included.

## What we can tell vendors

- The artwork is a one-colour vector logo, ready as PDF or SVG.
- Delivery in two batches of 1,000 is fine if it shortens the lead time.
- Payment terms are net 30 on invoice. We do not pay deposits.
- Quotes are due by 10 October 2026, as a reply to the request email.

## What only a person decides

- Choosing a vendor, placing an order, or agreeing to any price or condition.
- Changing the quantity, the specification or the delivery date.
- Deposits, contracts, NDAs, and samples that cost money.
